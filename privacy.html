Privacy Policy

Satisfying MarbleFlow respects user privacy. This application is intended for personal use to manage and upload YouTube videos.

Information We Access

This application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to access authorized YouTube account information and perform video upload operations.

How We Use Information

Information accessed through the YouTube Data API is used only to provide the application's video upload and management functionality.

Data Storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally on the application owner's computer.

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share user information with third parties.

Contact

For privacy questions, please contact the application owner through their Google account.
